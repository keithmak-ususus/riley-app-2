# Log something that happened earlier, and edit an entry's time
Labels: feature, P1

## Why
People often forget to log until 20 minutes later, and a wrong time breaks the trip planner.

## What
- Long-press any log button to choose "now / 5 / 10 / 15 / 30 min ago / pick a time"
- In the Today timeline, tap a row to edit its time (and its grams, for meals)
- The trip planner and stats keep working correctly with out-of-order entries

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] It's easy to discover (an "earlier?" hint on first use)
- [ ] Phone tapping stays one-tap for "now"
- [ ] It works on the iPad picker flow too
