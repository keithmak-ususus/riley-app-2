# Enrichment rotation and a "load before crating" checklist
Labels: feature, guidelines, P3

## Why
GUIDELINES.md §3: chews, puzzle feeders, Lickimat, snuffle mat and two Kongs on rotation. Crating works best with enrichment loaded first.

## What
- An enrichment list, showing what was used today and when each item was last washed
- A suggestion for the next item in the rotation
- Before a crate session, a quick prompt: "Kong or Lickimat loaded?"
- The safe-swap reminder until "Drop" is marked as learned

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] One tap to log the item used
- [ ] Works for sitters (once roles exist)
