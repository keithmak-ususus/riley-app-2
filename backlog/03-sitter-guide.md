# Sitter guide page
Labels: feature, P2

## Why
A sitter needs Riley's routine on one screen: what to feed, when he goes out, what to say, and who to call.

## What
A read-only page (a tab for sitters, and a link in Settings for owners). It's generated from settings and `GUIDELINES.md`, plus a free-text "Quirks & notes" field that owners can edit.
- Meals: times, grams per meal, food brand
- Toilet: every N minutes, plus the triggers (waking, eating, play, excitement) and the "Outside / Busy busy / Good" routine
- Bedtime routine and crate notes
- Vet name, phone and address, an emergency vet, and the owners' phone numbers
- Never tell him off. Enzymatic cleaner for accidents

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Readable at phone size and printable (print CSS)
- [ ] Figures update automatically when settings change (for example, after dropping to 3 meals)
