# Weekly trends and a vet summary export
Labels: feature, P3

## Why
Useful for seeing progress, and for handing the vet facts rather than memories.

## What
- Trends: accidents per day, outside %, sleep per day, food grams vs target, and weight curve (last 4 to 8 weeks)
- A "Vet summary": weight history, treatments with products and dates, recent accidents and stool notes, exported as a PDF or shared via the Android share sheet
- A full data export (JSON) as a backup

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Charts follow DESIGN.md
- [ ] The export works offline
- [ ] The export contains no household code or secrets
