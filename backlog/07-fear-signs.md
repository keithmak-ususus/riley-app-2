# Fear and anxiety signs: reference page and logging
Labels: feature, guidelines, P3

## Why
Kate's plan (GUIDELINES.md §5) lists fear signs. Spotting a pattern early helps socialisation, and accidents right after a stressful event may be fear-related.

## What
- A reference page of the signs, grouped (face, sounds, out of context, posture, other)
- When a socialisation exposure is logged as "unsure", let people tick which signs they saw
- The coach flags repeated triggers, and accidents within 15 minutes of an "unsure" exposure

- [ ] The wording matches GUIDELINES.md §5
- [ ] Coach suggestions cite "Riley's puppy plan"
